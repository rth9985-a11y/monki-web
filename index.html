OUR MISSION

Your biometrics are your own. No cloud. No prying eyes. All on your device. The Monki v1 is designed to move with its wearer, and our flexible design is noticeable only when necessary, with no compromise to performance .

  • Monki's mission is to bring personal bio-data back to the user.
  • With data brokers becoming common place, it feels as if we have come to ignore who has access to our data.
  • Monki puts bleeding edge technology in the users hands with no catch. No subscriptions. No cloud.
  • We believe that quality technology should not come at the cost of privacy or financial subscription dependency
