--- GENERAL PCB DESIGN NOTES ---



--- POWER ---
- 100nF caps near VDD
	- decouples the power pins from sudden changes in voltage

--- Analog Voltage on the MCU ---
- To convert analog to digital and digital to analog, there needs to be a refernce voltage to get a sense of the range of the signals
- IN ADC
	- The voltage is quantized to 2^resolution voltage points
	- We need maximum value (say 3.3v) and a minimum value (0v) to represent the signal
	- VREF+ would be the maximum signal and would be the analog equivalent to the digital 2^(MAX_VAL)
	- VREF- would be the minimum signal and would represent the digital 2^(MIN_VAL) [almost always a digital 0]
- We need a voltage reference for this


--- Op-amps, Why? ---
- Before using an ADC to convert a signal, it must be at the proper voltage
- VREF+/- determine the voltage range the op-amp can amplify before saturating
- AMPLIFICATION
	- E.g. 
		- Say that you have a microphone signal that sits between -10mV and +10mV
		- The ADC is asking for 3.3v to convert the analog signal 
		- Need an op-amp to amplify the analog signal such that it is within the ADC range!

- BUFFERING
	- High output impedance = not supplying a lot of current
	- Impedance buffering: Supply more or less current from one stage of a circuit to the next
	- e.g.
		- A sensor supplies changing voltage but very little current
		- The next stage in a circuit needs more current to operate
		- To increase the current while maintaining the voltage readings [ V_out = V_in ]
	- The high-level mechanism is as follows:
		1. The V+ is connected to the output of the sensor voltage V- is fed by the V_out
		2. The load (current needed by the next stage) is drawn from the output voltage
		3. The output voltage consequentially drops
		4. The op-amp's job is to keep V+ and V- the same
			- V_out connected to v- thus resulting in a difference between V+ and V-
			- Op-amp executes
		5. The result is that the load receives the proper amount of current and [ V_out = V_in ] 
- FILTERING
	- Anti-aliasing filters are used to maintain signal integrity and preventing folding back of frequencies
	- e.g.
		- X_c = \frac{1}{2pifC}
