Overview

Your biometrics are your own. No cloud. No prying eyes. All on your device. Monki v1 is designed to move with its wearer. Our flexible design is noticeable only when necessary with no compromise to performance .

Specifications

  • The clear loop is the TPU/silicone shell around the wrist.
  • The amber ribbon is the flex PCB. It runs the full loop, mid-stack.
  • The dot-matrix strip on top is the flexible OLED.
  • The battery and the optical heart-rate sensor sit on the skin side.
  • Disassemble pulls every part apart. Labels names each part.
